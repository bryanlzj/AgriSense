**Project Title:** AgriSense: IoT-Driven Early Warning System for Weather and Pest Risk Management

**Introduction**

**AgriSense** is an intelligent agricultural decision-support tool that combines **IoT, machine learning**, and **mobile computing** to deliver real-time early warnings about pest risks and weather changes. By deploying a network of sensors across farmland, it captures vital parameters such as **temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light intensity**. This data is transmitted to a cloud-based platform where predictive algorithms analyze it to forecast risks.

The system then delivers **real-time alerts and recommendations** through a mobile application tailored for farmers. The platform is designed to be scalable and adaptable to various crop types and geographical conditions, making it ideal for Malaysian agricultural settings. Additionally, the system promotes data-driven decision-making and reduces dependency on manual inspection and excessive pesticide use.

**Problem Statements**

- **Unpredictable Weather Patterns**: Malaysia's tropical climate causes frequent and erratic weather conditions, including heavy rains and high humidity, which can damage crops and reduce yield without timely mitigation.
- **Pest and Disease Outbreaks**: Farmers often lack timely and accurate information about potential pest infestations or crop diseases, leading to severe losses and increased reliance on reactive pesticide use.
- **Lack of Integrated Monitoring Systems**: Most existing systems in small- to medium-scale farms are fragmented or manual, resulting in inefficiencies in data collection, interpretation, and response.
- **Limited Accessibility to Real-time Decision Support**: Many farmers, especially in rural areas, lack access to real-time, user-friendly platforms that convert raw data into actionable insights for risk management.

**Project Objectives**

- To **design and develop** an IoT-enabled early warning system that collects real-time environmental data relevant to weather and pest risk factors.
- To **integrate machine learning models** for predictive analysis of pest outbreaks and weather anomalies based on the collected sensor data.
- To **create a mobile application** that provides farmers with real-time alerts, visual dashboards, and recommendations for preventive action.
- To **validate the system's accuracy and usability** through testing in a simulated or small-scale agricultural environment in Malaysia.

**Scope**

- **Hardware**: Design and deploy a basic IoT sensor node setup for capturing weather and soil data.
- **Software**: Develop a backend server for data aggregation and processing; implement machine learning models for forecasting.
- **Mobile App**: Build a mobile interface that displays predictions, environmental stats, and alerts.
- **Data**: Utilize publicly available or partner-provided agricultural datasets for training models (e.g., pest population trends, historical weather patterns).
- **Testing**: Simulate environmental conditions to test and refine system performance.
- **Target Audience**: Farmers, agritech stakeholders, agriculture officers, and Computer Science students.
